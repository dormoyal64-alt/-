2. What the service is

SeeTuned is a display-personalisation and viewing-comfort tool. It uses short on-screen visual checks to choose text size, contrast, colour and sharpness settings that are comfortable for you, and guides you to your device's accessibility settings. It also includes a camera magnifier and an enhanced photo and video viewer.

SeeTuned is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, screen for, monitor or treat any condition, and it does not provide a prescription for glasses or contact lenses. It does not replace an eye examination by an optometrist or ophthalmologist. Results are shown as display settings (for example "Recommended text size: 150%"), not as medical measurements. See the Medical Disclaimer.

You need a device with a screen (phone, tablet or computer), an up-to-date browser, and an internet connection to sign in and to buy. A camera is optional.
