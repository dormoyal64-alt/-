Privacy Policy

Version 2026-09-28 · Last updated 2026-09-29

This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with and what your rights are. It is written under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law 5741-1981 (including Amendment 13) and, where it applies to you, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR.

In short

  • Your visual-check results stay on your device. They are processed and stored only there and are never sent to our servers.
  • Our server stores only account and subscription data: e-mail, a password hash, language, your acceptance of the terms, subscription and invoice records, and an audit log.
  • The camera works only on your device. No images or video are saved or uploaded.
  • One essential cookie keeps you signed in. No advertising, no analytics and no trackers.
  • Payments are made with PayPlus or Paddle. We never see your card number.

2. Data that stays on your device

Your answers to the checks, the results, your display profile and the settings derived from them are processed in your browser and stored in its local storage (localStorage) on your device. They are never sent to our servers, and we have no access to them.

  • This information may say something about your eyesight, which is why it stays with you. Anyone with access to your device may be able to see it, so we recommend locking your device.
  • It is not synchronised between devices, and we cannot restore it.
  • You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data for SeeTuned in your browser or device settings. Deleting your account with us does not delete data stored on your device.

4. Purposes and legal basis

  • Providing the service and managing your account and subscription – necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • Invoices and legally required notices and reminders – to meet our obligations under tax and consumer-protection law.
  • Security, fraud prevention and one free trial per e-mail – our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising or build marketing profiles about you. We send marketing e-mails only if you explicitly agree, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Without such consent we send only service messages, such as invoices, reminders and cancellation confirmations.

5. Do you have to give us data?

You are not legally required to give us any information. However, without an e-mail address and password you cannot open an account, and without payment details (which you give to the payment provider, not to us) you cannot buy a plan.

6. Camera

  • The camera is used only for the magnifier and for optional measurement of your distance from the screen.
  • All processing happens on your device. Distance measurement uses Google's MediaPipe component, which runs inside your browser; its usage telemetry is disabled.
  • Camera images and video are never saved or uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Your browser asks for your permission before the camera is turned on. You can refuse or withdraw permission at any time, and the rest of the service keeps working.

7. Cookies and local storage

  • We use one essential cookie: an HttpOnly session cookie that keeps you signed in. Without it you cannot sign in.
  • There are no advertising cookies, no analytics tools and no third-party trackers.
  • Your browser's local storage keeps your results and settings on your device, as described above.
  • The PayPlus and Paddle payment pages may use their own cookies under their own privacy policies.

  • Paddle – Merchant of Record for purchases outside Israel. Paddle collects purchase and payment details itself and processes them under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting provider – [[HOSTING_PROVIDER]].
  • E-mail delivery provider – [[EMAIL_PROVIDER]], to send service messages.
  • Professional advisers bound by confidentiality (such as accountants and lawyers), and authorities where the law requires it.

These providers receive only the data they need to provide their service. None of them receive your check results, because we never receive them either.

9. Transfers outside Israel

Some providers, such as Paddle, operate outside Israel, so data such as your e-mail address and purchase details may be transferred to other countries. We transfer data abroad only in line with the Israeli Privacy Protection (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations 5761-2001 and, where the GDPR applies, its requirements. Israel has an EU adequacy decision, so transfers from the EU to Israel are permitted.

10. How long we keep data

  • Account details, subscription records and the audit log – for as long as your account exists.
  • Session tokens – until you sign out or they expire.
  • When you delete your account we delete all your data, and keep only:
    • invoice records that the law requires us to keep, not linked to your account, for the period required by tax law (generally 7 years);
    • a SHA-256 hash of your e-mail address, so that a second free trial is not given to the same address. The hash does not let us read your address; it only lets us recognise the same address if it signs up again.
  • The payment providers keep their own records under their own legal obligations.

11. Your rights

  • Access – ask to see the data we hold about you on our server (section 13 of the Privacy Protection Law).
  • Correction and deletion – ask us to correct or delete data that is inaccurate, incomplete, unclear or out of date (section 14).
  • Delete your account – at any time, on the account page in the app.
  • If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the rights to erasure, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To make a request, write to [[CONTACT_EMAIL]]. We may ask you to verify your identity. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority or to the data-protection authority in your country.

12. Security

  • All communication with the site is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored only as scrypt hashes, and session tokens are stored hashed.
  • The session cookie is HttpOnly, and the site is protected by a strict Content Security Policy (CSP).
  • Card details are entered only on the payment providers' pages and never pass through us.
  • Access to our systems is limited to those who need it, and sensitive actions are logged.

No system is completely secure. If a serious security incident occurs, we will act and notify as the law requires.

13. Children

The service is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data about minors. If we learn that an account was opened for a minor, we will delete it.
