Cancellation & Refund Policy

Version 2026-09-28 · Last updated 2026-09-29

This policy explains how to cancel, when cancellation takes effect and how refunds work. It supplements the Terms of Service and does not limit your rights under the Israeli Consumer Protection Law 5741-1981 or the consumer law of your country.

In short

  • Cancel with one click on the account page in the app. No cancellation fee.
  • Cancel within 14 days of your first payment: the plan ends immediately and you get a full refund.
  • After that: renewal stops and you keep access until the end of the paid period.
  • In Israel, the 3-month and 12-month plans do not renew automatically (outside Israel, see section 8).
  • A cancellation confirmation with a reference number is sent by e-mail.

1. How to cancel

You can give notice of cancellation in any of these ways:

  • In the app, with one click – on the account page.
  • On the website – through the "Cancel subscription" link on the home page and at the bottom of every page.
  • By e-mail – [[CONTACT_EMAIL]].
  • By phone – [[PHONE]].
  • By post – [[ADDRESS]].

If you cancel by e-mail, phone or post, give the e-mail address of your account and, if you wish, the date on which cancellation should take effect. You never have to speak to an agent to cancel. Deleting your account also cancels your subscription.

2. When cancellation takes effect

  • Within 14 days of your first payment – cancellation takes effect immediately, paid features stop and you get a full refund.
  • After 14 days – by default renewal stops immediately, there are no further charges, and you keep access until the end of the paid period.
  • If you want cancellation to take effect sooner – write to us or call. We will end the plan within 3 business days at most and refund the pro-rata part of your payment for the unused period.

After every cancellation we immediately e-mail you a confirmation with the cancellation date and a reference number.

3. Refunds

  • No cancellation fee, on any plan.
  • Refunds go back to the payment method you used, within 7 business days of cancellation. When the credit appears depends on your card issuer.
  • For purchases outside Israel, refunds are made through Paddle, the Merchant of Record.

4. Monthly plan

  • Renews automatically every month until you cancel. A reminder e-mail is sent before each charge.
  • After you cancel there are no further charges.

5. 3-month and 12-month plans

  • These plans are prepaid for a fixed term and do not renew automatically, so you do not need to cancel to avoid another charge.
  • Notices before the term ends:
    • about 60 days before the end (and no later than 30 days before it) – a separate written notice of the end date. For the 3-month plan it is sent during the first month;
    • if you have given us a mobile number – also an SMS at least 21 days before the end;
    • 7 days before the end – a reminder.
  • Renewal is possible only with your explicit consent and a new payment. A renewal is a new purchase, so the 14 days for a full refund start again from the renewal payment.
  • More than 14 days after payment, you keep access until the end of the term, or you can ask for early termination with a pro-rata refund, as described above.

6. Free trial

The 30-day trial involves no payment and no card details, and nothing is charged when it ends. You therefore do not need to cancel it.

7. Extended cancellation right

If you are a senior citizen (aged 65 or over), a person with a disability or a new immigrant, Israeli law may give you the right to cancel a distance sale within 4 months, where the transaction involved a conversation between us and you (for example by phone). In such cases we will honour that right and refund you as the law requires, with no cancellation fee. We may ask for proof of eligibility.

8. Purchases outside Israel

For purchases through Paddle, Paddle is the Merchant of Record and makes the refunds. The commitments in this policy also apply to these purchases, in addition to your rights under Paddle's buyer terms and the law of your country (for example the 14-day right of withdrawal in the EU and the UK). Outside Israel (purchases through Paddle, in US dollars) all plans — monthly, 3-month and 12-month — renew automatically at the end of each term until you cancel. We e-mail you a reminder before a 3- or 12-month plan renews, and you can cancel at any time in one click; access continues until the end of the paid term.
