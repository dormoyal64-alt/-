Accessibility Statement

Version 2026-09-28 · Last updated 2026-09-29

SeeTuned is built especially for people who find screens hard to read, so accessibility is at the heart of the product. This statement describes the accessibility of the website and the app, known limitations, and how to contact us.

1. Standard and conformance level

  • The website and the app were designed and built under the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations 5773-2013 (regulation 35), made under the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law 5758-1998.
  • Conformance level: AA under Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on the W3C's WCAG 2.0 guidelines.
  • Our own target is stricter: WCAG 2.1 level AA, and later WCAG 2.2.
  • Known limitations are listed below. We keep testing and improving.

2. Accessibility features of the website

  • Pages structured with headings and landmarks, and a "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page;
  • full keyboard navigation with a clearly visible focus indicator;
  • 18-pixel base text size, and browser zoom to 200% and beyond without loss of content;
  • colour contrast of at least level AA, in light and dark mode (following your device setting);
  • large touch targets (at least 48 pixels) in menus;
  • language and text direction declared on every page, so screen readers read Hebrew and English correctly;
  • layouts adapted to phones, tablets and computers;
  • no flashing content, no auto-playing media and no time limits on website pages.

3. Accessibility features of the app

  • the app itself adapts text size, contrast and colours to your results;
  • a camera magnifier and an enhanced photo and video viewer;
  • a step-by-step guide to turning on your device's accessibility settings (larger text, zoom, colour filters, screen reader);
  • large buttons with text labels, with support for keyboard navigation and screen readers;
  • plain-language instructions, and the option to stop a check at any point;
  • Hebrew and English.

4. Known limitations and alternatives

  • The visual checks rely on sight by their nature, so they cannot be fully completed with a screen reader. Alternative: contact the accessibility coordinator and we will help you choose display settings without the checks.
  • Symbol sizes on check screens are set on purpose from the screen calibration, so browser zoom or device zoom during a check may affect the result. Outside the check screens you can zoom as usual.
  • The colour checks depend on telling colours apart. The result is also shown in text.
  • The camera is needed for the magnifier and the optional distance measurement. The rest of the service works without a camera.
  • The payment pages of PayPlus and Paddle are run by third parties, and their accessibility is outside our control. If you run into difficulty, contact us and we will help you complete your purchase.
  • Older browsers may not support some features. We recommend an up-to-date browser.

5. Physical accessibility

The service is provided online only; we have no office or service centre open to the public.
