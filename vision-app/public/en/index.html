Sound familiar?

Your phone is full of excellent accessibility settings – but it is hard to know which ones suit your eyes.

  • Text that is too small

    You pinch, zoom and hold the phone closer and further away – and still strain to read messages and menus.

  • Glare and washed-out screens

    Too bright in the evening, too pale in the sun, and thin letters that fade into the background.

  • Colours that blend together

    Red and green look alike on a chart, a map or a button – and important details get lost.

  • Tired eyes

    After a few minutes of reading on screen your eyes feel tired, and reading becomes slow and unpleasant.

How it works

Three steps – and most of the work happens only the first time.

  1. Short visual checks

    Simple on-screen comfort checks, one eye at a time: how large text needs to be, how much contrast and sharpness feel comfortable, and which colours are easy for you to tell apart. No special equipment.

    About 5 minutes

  2. Your personal profile

    Your results become clear display settings: a recommended text size, contrast level, sharpness and, if needed, a colour filter. No medical numbers and no diagnoses – and the profile stays on your device.

  3. Your whole device, tuned

    Exact step-by-step guidance for the accessibility settings of your iPhone, iPad or Android device – so every app looks better. And inside SeeTuned: photos, videos, a live magnifier and a reader, all following your profile.

What you get

Everything you need to make your screen easier and more comfortable to read.

  • Text that fits you

    Size, weight, line and letter spacing – set to what you find comfortable to read.

  • Contrast and sharpness

    Stronger contrast and clearer edges, without the glare.

  • Colour filters

    Filters that make colours you find hard to separate easier to tell apart.

  • Photos and videos

    An enhanced viewer for the photos and videos on your device, following your profile.

  • Live magnifier

    Point the camera at a menu, a label or a sign – and see it large and clear.

  • Comfortable reader

    Long text shown at the size, weight and colours you find comfortable for longer reading.

  • Whole-device settings

    Exact guidance for your device's built-in accessibility settings, so your other apps fit you too.

  • A profile for everyone

    Several family members on one tablet – each with their own profile, one tap to switch.

What SeeTuned is – and isn't

We want you to know exactly what you are getting.

SeeTuned is

  • A display-personalisation and viewing-comfort tool.
  • Short visual checks that help you find settings that are easier for you to read.
  • Exact guidance for your device's own accessibility settings.
  • Private: your check results and profile stay on your device and are not sent to us.

SeeTuned is not

  • Not a medical device. It does not diagnose, screen for, monitor or treat any condition.
  • Not a prescription for glasses or contact lenses, and not a replacement for an eye examination by an optometrist or ophthalmologist.
  • Not a fix for blur: a normal screen cannot optically correct blurry vision the way glasses do. We tune size, contrast, colour and sharpness so reading is more comfortable.

We recommend regular eye checks with an optometrist or ophthalmologist. If you notice sudden changes in your vision, seek care promptly.

Frequently asked questions

Does SeeTuned replace an eye examination?

No. SeeTuned is a display-personalisation and viewing-comfort tool. Its short visual checks help you choose a text size, contrast, colour and sharpness that are comfortable for you. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose and does not provide a prescription, and it does not replace an examination by an optometrist or ophthalmologist. If you notice sudden changes in your vision, seek care promptly.

What happens to the data about my eyes?

Your check results and profiles are stored only on your device and are not sent to our servers. We keep only your account and subscription details, such as your email address and plan. You can delete your profiles and your whole account at any time. Full details are in our privacy policy.

How does the free month work?

Sign up with an email address and a password – no card required – and get 30 days with every feature. Nothing is charged automatically when the trial ends: if you want to continue, you choose a plan and pay. If not, you simply stop.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Any time, in one click and with no cancellation fee: on the account page in the app, or through the “Cancel subscription” link at the bottom of every page on this site. On the monthly plan, automatic renewal stops and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. The 3-month and yearly plans do not renew automatically anyway. Cancelling within 14 days of your first charge gives you a full refund. Details are in our cancellation policy.

Which devices does it work on?

SeeTuned runs in the browser on phones and tablets: iPhone and iPad (in Safari) and Android devices (in Chrome), on a recent version. There is nothing to download from an app store – you can add it to your home screen and use it like an app. The settings guidance matches your type of device.

I wear glasses. Should I do the checks with them?

Yes. Do the checks the way you usually look at your phone – with your reading glasses or contact lenses if you use them. SeeTuned does not replace glasses and does not work out a prescription. If you sometimes use your device with glasses and sometimes without, you can create two profiles and switch between them.

Can we have several profiles on a family tablet?

Yes. Each person in the household can do the checks and save their own profile on the same device, then switch between profiles in the app. Profiles are stored on the device itself. Note that the device's own accessibility settings (such as the system text size) are shared by everyone who uses it.

Is it suitable for children?

An adult opens the account. Children can use a profile on the family device with an adult's help, but SeeTuned is not designed to check children's vision and does not replace an eye examination. We recommend that an optometrist or ophthalmologist checks children's eyes regularly.

Can a screen fix blurry vision?

No. A normal screen cannot optically correct blur the way glasses or contact lenses do. What we do instead: enlarge text, strengthen contrast, tune colours and sharpen lines and details – so reading and seeing are easier and more comfortable. If everything still looks blurry after tuning, we recommend seeing an optometrist or ophthalmologist.

Does SeeTuned change my phone's settings by itself?

No – browsers do not let a website or web app change system settings, which is good for your security. Instead, SeeTuned gives you exact step-by-step guidance, based on your profile, for your device's built-in accessibility settings: text size, bold text, contrast, colour filters and zoom. The change itself takes a few taps, and every app on the device benefits from it.
